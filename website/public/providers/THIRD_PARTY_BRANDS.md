# Third-party provider brands

Provider product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their presence in PlanOnce indicates compatibility only and does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation.

Bundled SVGs are locally hosted, theme-safe (light/dark pair), deterministic, and free of runtime CDN requests.

- **Anthropic (Claude Code), OpenAI (Codex), Google Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf**: glyph path data sourced from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0) and rendered into 64×64 rounded brand cards with original brand palette preserved. See simple-icons license.
- **Cursor, Opencode, Cline, Kilo Code, Kiro, Roo Code, Qwen Code, Goose, OpenHands**: PlanOnce-authored compatibility marks. Crafted to be faithful, high-fidelity representations with correct brand hues and solid fills (not thin strokes), boxed in 64×64 rounded surfaces for consistent layout. Provider trademarks remain with owners. Replace only with an approved official asset.

When an approved official asset is added, update `src/data/provider-brands.ts` with its `sourceUrl`, `sourceType`, and `licenseOrTrademarkNote` and preserve `preserveBrandColors: true`.

These third-party names and any future official brand assets are not licensed under PlanOnce's software license.
